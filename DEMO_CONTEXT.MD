Case Study 1: Sentinel RadarSubtitle: Multi-Agent Lease Portfolio AnalyserThe Bottleneck: In Commercial Real Estate, missing a single clause in a dense lease document results in massive "financial leakage" and liability. Existing legacy systems act as passive "Systems of Record" requiring manual data entry. Standard AI extraction tools are untrustworthy for this task because they hallucinate facts without an audit trail, making them unusable for enterprise asset managers who need to confidently track complex variables like uncapped service charges or upward-only rent reviews.The Architecture:We engineered a deterministic, three-agent pipeline to replace single-prompt guessing for an early-stage PropTech Operating System:Layer 1 (Extraction): A high-speed extraction model parses dense PDFs into structured JSON, isolating financials, timelines, and 12 specific clause categories.Layer 2 (Validation): A secondary advanced reasoning agent conducts a 5-point quality audit, cross-referencing arithmetic and timeline logic, acting as an automated Senior Surveyor to catch hallucinated data.Layer 3 (Synthesis): A macro-analysis model runs cross-lease portfolio analytics to detect term variances between properties.Key Features Delivered:Deterministic Risk Scoring: Replaced opaque AI "scores" with a formula-driven, auditable risk matrix (e.g., Base 50 + 25 points for no break clause + 10 points for uncapped liability).Clause Comparison Variance: Interactive, side-by-side matrices that instantly highlight outlier clauses across multi-property portfolios.Quantified ROI Calculator: Automatically calculates the exact monetary value of renegotiating specific flagged clauses (e.g., turnover rent optimization, admin fee reduction).Graceful Degradation: The pipeline is fault-tolerant; if the validation layer experiences API timeouts, extraction continues, ensuring the client's demo never hard-crashes.Case Study 2: Voice Commander (Sidecar Integration)Subtitle: AI-Native CRE Deal Intelligence PWAThe Bottleneck: A rapidly scaling Commercial Real Estate CRM startup faced a velocity gap. Their core engineering team was locked into building backend infrastructure, but users needed a mobile-first field tool to stop deals from falling through the cracks. They needed to bridge the gap between "field intelligence" and "CRM entry" without forcing their engineers to refactor the core legacy database.The Architecture:We deployed a "Sidecar" PWA (Progressive Web App) architecture that operates alongside the core application.Audio Ingestion: Integrated enterprise-grade speech-to-text for instant voice memo transcription.Multimodal Extraction: Utilized advanced multimodal LLMs to extract structured 10-point JSON deal data from both spoken audio and user-uploaded photos of "For Lease" signs.Market Grounding: Connected a search-grounded AI agent to pull live, localized market comps based on the extracted property address.Key Features Delivered:Zero-Friction CRM Entry: Converts a rambling 60-second voice memo into structured data instantly.Missing Field Detection ("The Coach"): A quality-gate algorithm that flags critical missing data (like Square Footage or Rent) and prompts the broker before saving.Smart Badges: Keyword triggers automatically tag deals with visual priority badges (e.g., "Urgent," "Motivated Seller").Automated Document Drafting: One-click client-side PDF generation of Letters of Intent (LOIs) and follow-up emails using extracted variables.Case Study 3: Forensic Anti-Cheat EngineSubtitle: Behavioral Telemetry for Technical InterviewsThe Bottleneck: Generative AI tools have severely compromised technical recruitment. Current platforms either blanket-ban AI (which is unrealistic) or rely on invasive "Big Brother" proctoring that candidates despise. Furthermore, "black box" AI rejections create massive compliance liabilities under laws like NYC Local Law 144. A stealth-stage HR-Tech platform needed a defensible, audit-ready way to prove candidate integrity.The Architecture:We engineered an AI-resistant, 10-layer behavioral detection engine integrated natively into a cloud-based code editor.Telemetry Layer: A custom JavaScript hook tracks keystroke velocity, oscillation, return signatures, and linearity to distinguish between human cognition and AI transcription.Vision Layer: Uses automated vision AI on interval snapshots to check gaze direction and cross-reference physical behavior with code anomalies.AI Commentary: Synthesizes the raw telemetry data into real-time, plain-English commentary for the observing recruiter.Key Features Delivered:Adaptive Baselines: Calibrates a unique typing and correction baseline for each candidate in the first 60 seconds to eliminate false positives for naturally fast typists.Compound Event Triggers: Prevents false flags by requiring multiple layers to agree (e.g., "Focus Loss" + "Bulk Insert" + "0% Undo Ratio").Auto-Interrogation: Automatically locks the editor and generates targeted questions about suspicious code blocks, forcing the candidate to explain their logic live.Audit-Ready Forensics: Outputs a structured, highly defensible evidence chain and session replay that satisfies enterprise HR compliance requirements.Case Study 4: Dynamic Pricing IntelligenceSubtitle: Multi-Tenant F&B Data OrchestrationThe Bottleneck: The food and beverage industry suffers from a severe data silo problem. Operators rely on static "cost-plus" pricing because competitive market data is locked inside third-party delivery apps. A VC-backed pricing intelligence platform needed a way to ingest, normalize, and analyze chaotic competitor menus to provide actionable revenue opportunities to their users.The Architecture:We engineered a headless data ingestion and semantic mapping pipeline to bypass closed APIs and normalize unstructured data at scale.Automated Ingestion: Deployed headless browser scraping infrastructure on daily automated cron schedules.Semantic Normalization: Utilized advanced semantic embeddings with cosine similarity to map wildly inconsistent competitor menu categories (e.g., comparing "Handhelds" to "Sandwiches") into unified canonical categories.Multi-Tenant Isolation: Built a secure backend where competitor datasets, operator profiles, and alerts are strictly tenant-scoped.Key Features Delivered:Automated Gap Identification: An analytical engine that compares the operator's menu against the normalized market data, flagging specific items priced >10% below local averages.ROI Projection Calculator: Translates identified pricing gaps into concrete annual revenue projections based on operator volume, proving the software's ROI instantly.Asynchronous Alerting: An automated system that tracks 30-day and 90-day price trends and fires specific alerts when competitors alter pricing or remove items.
